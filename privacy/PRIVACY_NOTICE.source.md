# DriveBai Privacy Notice

Last updated 8 October 2026.

This notice describes what DriveBai collects about you, who sees it, how long it
is kept, and what you can do about it. It describes what the app and our systems
actually do today. Where something is coming but is not built yet, this notice
does not mention it, and it will be updated before it is.

DriveBai connects people who own cars with people who want to rent them, and in
some cases buy them. Because of that, some of your information is shown to the
other person in a rental or a sale. The sections below say exactly which.

---

## 1. What we collect, and why

### When you create an account

Your name, email address, phone number, and a password if you set one. Some
accounts sign in with an emailed code instead, in which case we hold no
password. A profile photo if you add one.

We need these to identify you, to let the other party in a rental know who they
are dealing with, and to contact you about a rental.

### When you get ready to rent as a driver

A photograph or scan of your driving licence, and any other document we ask you
to upload. These are stored as image files against your account.

We need them so a car owner can satisfy themselves that you are licensed before
handing over a car.

**Read this part carefully, because it surprises people.** When you send a
request for a car, the documents you have uploaded are shared with that car's
owner at that moment — not when they accept, but when you ask. The owner can
view them for as long as the request exists. If you request five cars and rent
none of them, five owners have seen your licence. If you upload a new document
later, it is added to the most recent request automatically.

We do it this way because an owner deciding whether to hand over a car wants to
see who is asking. We are aware this shares more, and earlier, than it needs to,
and we are looking at moving the sharing to the moment an owner accepts. Until
we do, this notice describes what happens now.

### When you list a car as an owner

The car's details, its photographs, the address or area where it is kept, and
its registration and insurance documents. The pickup address is shown to drivers
who are renting it, because they have to collect it.

### When you pay or get paid

Card payments and payouts are handled by Stripe. **We never see or store your
full card number.** Stripe holds that. We store a record that a payment
happened, the amount, the last four digits and the card brand so you can
recognise which card, and Stripe's own reference for it.

If you are an owner receiving payouts, Stripe collects identity and bank
information from you directly to meet financial regulations. That information is
held by Stripe under their own privacy policy, not by us. We hold your Stripe
account reference and whether your payout setup is complete.

### When you use the app

Messages you send in a rental's chat, which the other party can read. The
notifications we send you and whether they were opened. A token that lets us
send notifications to your phone. Basic technical records of requests to our
systems, including your device type and internet address, kept for security and
for diagnosing faults.

### Where you are — location

We collect location in three specific situations and at no other time.

**Centring a map.** When you are choosing where your car will be picked up, the
app can centre the map near you so you are not dragging a pin across the world.
This happens while you are on that screen and nothing is stored.

**At handover.** When you confirm you have picked a car up, and when you confirm
you have returned it, we record roughly where that happened. It is part of the
handover record, along with the photographs. You can see it; the other party is
not shown it, and support sees it only in the situation described below, where a
rental has ended unpaid. We delete it one year after the rental ends.

**When a rental has ended without payment and the car has not come back.** In
that situation, and only then, each time you open DriveBai we record roughly
where your phone is, so that the owner can get their car back. We tell you
before this starts, a banner stays on your screen for the whole time it is
happening, and you can see everything we have recorded in the app.

Some things that are true of all three:

- The location is **approximate — about a kilometre**. The app asks your phone
  only for a rough position, so your phone never gives us a precise one even if
  you have allowed precise location for DriveBai; and we round what arrives to
  roughly the nearest kilometre before storing it, so a precise position could
  not be stored even by mistake. We could not tell you which street you are on.
- We **never** collect location in the background. The app has to be open.
- We never collect it while your rental is paid up and running normally.
- We never collect speed, direction, or a route, and we never build a history of
  your movements.

Section 4 covers who sees location and for how long we keep it.

### If you are involved in an accident or a dispute

What you report to us, photographs you upload, and any signature you provide.

### If you buy or sell a car through DriveBai

Both parties' legal name and address go on the bill of sale, along with the
vehicle identification number, the odometer reading and both signatures, because
that is what a bill of sale is. Each party can see the other's driving licence
image as part of that transaction. The completed bill of sale is a record of the
sale and we keep it — see section 5.

## 2. What we do **not** do

- We do not sell your information to anyone.
- We do not use it for advertising, and we do not let anyone else use it for
  advertising.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising or
  analytics tracking in DriveBai.
- We do not collect your location in the background, ever.
- We do not store your full card number.
- We do not use your information to train machine-learning models.

## 3. Who else sees your information

### The other party in your rental or sale

A driver sees: the owner's name, profile photo, the car and its photographs, the
pickup address, and the chat.

An owner sees: the driver's name, profile photo, phone number, the documents the
driver has uploaded including their driving licence, the payment history for the
rental, and the chat. In a rental that has ended unpaid, an owner who asks
support may be told the **general area** — a neighbourhood — that the driver's
phone was last in. Never a street, never an address, never a map, and never
whether the driver allowed location at all.

In a sale, each party additionally sees the other's legal name, address,
signature and driving licence image, on the bill of sale.

### DriveBai support

Our support staff can see your account, your rentals, your payments and your
messages, in order to help. When a rental has ended unpaid, support can see the
approximate locations described above. **Every time a member of staff looks at
your location, that is recorded with their name, the time and their reason, and
you can read that record in the app.**

### Companies that do work for us

- **Stripe** — payments and payouts. They receive your name, email and payment
  details directly.
- **Apple** — delivering notifications to your phone, and looking up the name of
  a neighbourhood from a location. The lookup is done on your phone, and from the
  rounded position, so what Apple receives is no more precise than what we hold.
- **MailerSend or SendGrid** — sending emails.
- **Fly.io** — hosting our systems and database.
- **The United States National Highway Traffic Safety Administration** — we look
  up a vehicle identification number against their public database to check a
  car's details. This sends the vehicle number only, never anything about you.

### Police and legal requests

If a car is reported stolen we give the police what we hold, including
approximate locations, and we keep a record of having done so. We also respond to
valid legal process. If police or a court serve us with a written request to
preserve information for longer than our normal retention, we do, we log it, and
we review it every 90 days.

## 4. Location, in detail

Because location is the most sensitive thing we hold, this section says
everything about it in one place.

| | Collected when | Precision | Who can see it | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Map centring | while you are on the pickup-location screen | approximate | nobody — it is not stored | not stored |
| Handover | when you confirm a pickup or a return | approximate, about a kilometre | you; support only once a rental has ended unpaid; never the other party | 1 year after the rental ends |
| Overdue rental | each time you open the app, after a rental has ended unpaid with the car not returned, and after we have told you | approximate, about a kilometre | you, support with every look recorded, the owner only as a neighbourhood | 30 days after we stop collecting |

**When collection starts.** A full day after the rental ended with the car not
returned, and only after we have sent you the notification saying it is
starting. Not while a payment is merely late, and not during the grace period
you have to pay and continue — during that time your rental is still running.

**When collection stops.** As soon as any of these happens: you start a return
in the app, the return is confirmed, support closes the rental, or 30 days pass
from the first location we recorded. If the car has been reported stolen to
police, 90 days instead of 30.

Starting a return stops it for good. If the return is later cancelled or
withdrawn, collection does **not** simply resume: it can only start again if
our support team turns it back on deliberately, with a written reason, and we
notify you when they do.

**How long we keep it.** 30 days after collection stops. So the longest any
location can exist is 60 days from the first one, or 120 days where police are
involved. Beyond that only on a written preservation request from police or a
court.

**How to stop it.** Return the car in the app; collection stops immediately. Or
switch location off for DriveBai in your phone's Settings at any time, which
stops the app recording anything. Your rental is unaffected either way, what you
owe is unaffected, and we do not tell the owner what you chose.

**What you can see.** In the app, under Settings then Your data then Location:
every location we hold with its date and neighbourhood, every time support looked
and why, and a button to request deletion.

## 5. How long we keep things

| | |
|---|---|
| Your account and profile | while your account exists |
| Documents you upload, including your licence | while your account exists; deleted when you delete your account |

## 6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, under Settings. It asks you to type
DELETE and, if you have a password, to enter it.

**What is blocked.** You cannot delete while a rental is in progress, a car of
yours is out with a driver, or a payment is in flight. The app tells you what to
finish first, and each of those can be finished in the app.

**What deletion removes.** Your profile, your documents and their image files,
every location we hold about you — overdue-rental and handover alike — and your
ability to sign in. Your name is replaced with a placeholder on records that have
to survive. No location of any kind survives deletion of your account.

**What survives deletion, and why.**

- **Any balance you owe.** Deleting your account does not clear what you owe, and
  we keep enough to identify the debt and contact you about it.
- **Rentals, payments and payouts**, for the 7 years tax rules require, with your
  name replaced by a placeholder.
- **Bills of sale**, because they are the record of a car changing hands.
- **Your Stripe records.** Stripe holds its own copy of your customer record, and
  for owners the payout account with its identity verification. Deleting your
  DriveBai account does not delete those. Contact Stripe about theirs.
- **Messages you sent**, because deleting them would remove the other party's
  side of a conversation they may need.

## 7. Your choices

- **See what we hold.** Location is in the app under Your data. For anything
  else, ask us and we will provide it.
- **Correct it.** Edit your profile in the app, or ask us.
- **Delete it.** Section 6.
- **Turn location off.** Your phone's Settings, at any time. It changes nothing
  about your rental.
- **Turn notifications off.** Your phone's Settings. Note that some things we are
  required to tell you — that a location is being recorded, that a payment
  failed — will then only appear inside the app, so we recommend leaving
  notifications on.
- **Complain.** Contact us first. You can also complain to your state's attorney
  general.

---

## 8. Security

Information travels encrypted between your phone and our systems, and is
encrypted where it is stored. Your documents are served through links that
expire. Access to support tools requires an administrator account, and looking
at a driver's location is recorded against the member of staff who did it.

Nothing is perfect, and we will tell you promptly if something goes wrong with
information about you.

---

## 9. Children

DriveBai is for adults who hold a driving licence. We do not knowingly collect
anything about anyone under 18. If you believe we have, tell us and we will
delete it.

---

## 10. Changes

If we change how we use your information, we will update this notice and, where
the change matters, tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the
top always reflects the current version.
